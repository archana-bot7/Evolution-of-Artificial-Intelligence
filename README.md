# The Rise of Artificial Intelligence — Power BI Analysis

Power BI data analysis project exploring the growth and impact of Artificial Intelligence from **2018 to 2025**.

The analysis focuses on AI adoption, AI software revenue, global AI market value, estimated revenue increase, jobs created and eliminated, organizational adoption, productivity, and public/professional belief in AI.


## Dashboard Overview

### Dashboard 1 — AI Growth Overview

Covers:
- AI adoption by year
- AI software revenue vs. average net job loss
- Jobs eliminated vs. jobs created
- Global AI market value and estimated revenue increase
- Drivers of AI adoption
- Job Displacement Rate
- AI Adoption YoY Growth %
