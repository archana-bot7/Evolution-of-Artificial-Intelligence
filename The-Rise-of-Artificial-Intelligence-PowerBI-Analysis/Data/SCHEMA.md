# Data Schema

The report identifies the following fields in the Power BI table.

| Field | Category | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Year | Time | Year from 2018 to 2025 |
| AI Software Revenue | Growth | AI software revenue |
| Global AI Market Value | Growth | Global AI market value |
| Estimated Revenue Increase from AI | Growth | Estimated revenue increase attributed to AI |
| AI Contribution to GDP | Growth | AI contribution to GDP |
| AI Adoption (%) | Adoption | AI adoption measure |
| Organizations Using AI | Adoption | Organizations currently using AI |
| Organizations Planning to Implement AI | Adoption | Organizations planning AI implementation |
| Organizations Believing AI Provides Competitive Edge | Adoption | Organizations associating AI with competitive advantage |
| Companies Prioritizing AI in Strategy | Adoption | Companies prioritizing AI strategically |
| Estimated New Jobs Created by AI | Jobs | Estimated jobs created, in millions |
| Estimated Jobs Eliminated by AI | Jobs | Estimated jobs eliminated, in millions |
| Net Job Loss in the US | Jobs | Net US job-loss measure |
| Jobs at High Risk of Automation | Jobs | Jobs identified as highly exposed to automation |
| Expected Increase in Employee Productivity Due to AI | Belief / Impact | Expected productivity improvement |
| Marketers Believing AI Improves Email Revenue | Belief / Impact | Marketers' belief measure |
| Americans Using Voice Assistants | Belief / Impact | Americans using voice assistants |
| Medical Professionals Using AI for Diagnosis | Belief / Impact | Medical professionals using AI for diagnosis |

The report states that the table contains these growth, adoption, jobs, and belief-related fields. No additional column names have been invented here.
