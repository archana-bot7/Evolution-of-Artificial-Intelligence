# Data

The analysis uses one Power BI table:

`The Rise Of Artificial Intellegence2 (1)`

Each row represents one year from **2018 to 2025**, giving 8 yearly records.

## Main Data Groups

### Growth
- AI Software Revenue
- Global AI Market Value
- Estimated Revenue Increase from AI
- AI Contribution to GDP

### Adoption
- AI Adoption (%)
- Organizations Using AI
- Organizations Planning to Implement AI
- Organizations Believing AI Provides Competitive Edge
- Companies Prioritizing AI in Strategy

### Jobs
- Estimated New Jobs Created by AI
- Estimated Jobs Eliminated by AI
- Net Job Loss in the US
- Jobs at High Risk of Automation

### Belief / Impact
- Expected Increase in Employee Productivity Due to AI
- Marketers Believing AI Improves Email Revenue
- Americans Using Voice Assistants
- Medical Professionals Using AI for Diagnosis

The original source dataset was not uploaded separately with this report, so this repository does not fabricate or substitute a dataset.
