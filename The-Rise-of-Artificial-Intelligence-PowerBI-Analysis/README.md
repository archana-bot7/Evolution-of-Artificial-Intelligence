# The Rise of Artificial Intelligence — Power BI Analysis

Power BI data analysis project exploring the growth and impact of Artificial Intelligence from **2018 to 2025**.

The analysis focuses on AI adoption, AI software revenue, global AI market value, estimated revenue increase, jobs created and eliminated, organizational adoption, productivity, and public/professional belief in AI.

## Key Figures

- AI Adoption: **2.68** (sum across the report's yearly values)
- Global AI Market Value: **2,803.5B**
- Estimated Revenue Increase: **26.5T USD**
- Net Job Impact: **-0.75M**
- Period: **2018–2025**
- Years: **8**
- Dashboards: **2**

## Dashboard Overview

### Dashboard 1 — AI Growth Overview

Covers:
- AI adoption by year
- AI software revenue vs. average net job loss
- Jobs eliminated vs. jobs created
- Global AI market value and estimated revenue increase
- Drivers of AI adoption
- Job Displacement Rate
- AI Adoption YoY Growth %

### Dashboard 2 — Jobs, Belief and Impact

Covers:
- Drivers of estimated revenue increase
- Belief in AI across groups
- Organizations' use of AI by year
- Net Job Impact
- Average AI Adoption Rate
- Total Market Value
- Year-by-year summary
- Jobs created vs. eliminated by year

## Main Findings

- AI adoption increased every year and rose especially sharply in 2024–2025.
- Global AI market value increased sharply from **$400B in 2024 to $1.81T in 2025**.
- Estimated revenue increase from AI reached **$5.5T in 2025**.
- Across the full period, the report estimates **2.20M jobs eliminated** and **1.45M jobs created**, resulting in a net impact of about **-0.75M jobs**.
- Employee productivity expectations and organizational AI usage were identified as strong drivers of estimated revenue increase.
- Companies prioritizing AI in strategy showed the highest belief/priority measure among the groups analyzed.
